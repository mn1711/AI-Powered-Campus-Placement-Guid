Walmart Interview Experience(2026 off- campus)
 (Internship(2M+6M)+FTE)
I got this opportunity through WALMART CODEHERS 2025-2026.

About CodeHers 2025:
CodeHers is an exclusive coding challenge organized by Walmart Global Tech India, designed to empower female engineering students and make technology careers more accessible. This two-day event attracts over 1,25,000+ registrations annually and provides a platform for students to showcase their coding skills, compete with peers, and unlock exciting career opportunities with one of the world’s leading tech companies.

Eligibility Criteria:
1. Degrees Eligible:
B.E/B.Tech (Full-time) – Batch of 2025 & 2026.
Dual Degree (B.Tech + M.Tech) – Batch of 2025 & 2026.
M.E/M.Tech (Full-time) – Batch of 2025 & 2026.
2. Branches Eligible:
Circuit Branches Only: CS, IT, ECE, EE, EEE.
3. Academic Requirement:
Minimum 7.0 CGPA.

Competition Structure:
The challenge consists of three levels:
1. Level 1 – MCQ Challenge:
25 objective questions to test computer fundamentals.
Time limit: 25 minutes.
2. Level 2 – Coding Challenge:
2 problem statements to solve in 90 minutes.
Focus on logical coding solutions.
3. Level 3 – Final Evaluation:
Cumulative scores from Levels 1 & 2 will be considered.
Top performers will be shortlisted for interviews.

Result Declaration Process:
Each coding question carries 100 marks (equal weightage for private and public test cases).
It took almost 3 months for the completion of whole process.

Internship Interview Experience(2M-Technical+Hr):
Firstly, I introduced myself to the interviewer. He then asked about the college and courses.
He asked the Cs fundamentals like Oops concepts, Exceptional Handling code, All Access modifiers in detail, How are objects created, where are they stored and instantiation , Slow-fast pointers, Double Linked List and its real life applications , **Pointer and its real life applications, Heaps and applications of heaps, Stack application, Trees ,Graph and types of trees and then moved to DSA

Coding:
Ques 1: Detect loop in a Double Linked List
This is a standard striver question so i managed to do it using slow-fast pointer approach. This is a medium level question.

Ques 2:Heaps Question (medium level) and a follow up question.

Finally, for 10 min he asked basic hr questions.
The interview went for 50-55 minutes and then after 45 days I got a mail that I was selected.
Later,6M+FTE got converted with the collective peformance.

Tips for cracking interview
1. Along with good DSA and technical knowledge, you should have strong communication skills.
2. Be confident in your answers and try to make conversation light.
3. Have knowledge of the background of the company .
4. Do not include words that you are not confident about, as your next question will likely start from that topic.
5. Be thorough with your resume.
