MoneyView Campus Placement Experience

Overview and Eligibility Criteria
MoneyView conducted a parallel pool recruitment drive across four NITs (Jamshedpur, Rourkela, Trichy, and Raipur).
● Cutoff: Minimum CPI of 8.0.
● Eligible Branches: Open to all branches for the OA; final interview shortlists were restricted to CS, IT, and ECE students.

Round 1: Online Assessment (OA)
The OA evaluated core knowledge, quantitative speed, and algorithmic problem-solving under tight time limits.
● Aptitude & Core MCQs: Standard time-bound aptitude questions alongside foundational OS/DBMS MCQs.
● Coding Section:
1. A story-based Fibonacci variant (LeetCode Easy) where F(0) = 0.
2. A graph-based DFS question. I solved it via DSU (Disjoint Set Union).
● My Performance: I successfully solved both coding questions and completed nearly 60% of the MCQs accurately.
Shortlist Outcome: Solving at least one coding question with a solid MCQ/Aptitude score was the baseline to progress. From our college alone, 30 students moved on to the next round.

The Interview Phase

Technical Interview 1 (1 Hour)
● Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA):
○ Linked List Cycle Detection: A modified variant where node data values could repeat. Cycle detection had to be calculated explicitly by validation of node addresses/references rather than node values.
○ 3Sum Problem: A standard multi-pointer array problem to find unique triplets matching a target sum.
● Language Specifics (Java): As Java was my primary coding language, I was asked to write a functional implementation of parallel running threads.
● OOPs: Detailed conceptual exploration of standard Object-Oriented Programming paradigms.

Technical Interview 2: System Design and Architecture (1 Hour)
A completely theoretical round with no coding involved.
● HashMap Internals: I was asked to describe the internal working of a HashMap and what and how collision happens.
● Project Scaling: The interviewer isolated a project from my resume and analyzed architectural behavior under stress tests, such as managing a sudden influx of one million active users and some other concepts.

HR Interview (45 Minutes)
● Guesstimate: Estimating the annual consumption of toothpaste tubes within our college hostel.
● Behavioral: Deciding between Virat Kohli or Hrithik Roshan as a role model, supported by core logical reasoning.
● Situational: Discussing previous campus placement interviews and evaluating reasons for not being selected.
● The Result: The HR manager verbally confirmed my final selection at the end of the conversation.

Recommended Resources
● Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA): Striver’s A-Z DSA Sheet covers all critical interview patterns. Nothing comes apart from these in Interviews (exception DE Shaw)
● Online Assessments (OA): TLE Eliminators CP Sheets up to a 1400 is more than enough.
● Aptitude: CareerRide Aptitude Playlist for shortcut methodologies.
● Operating Systems (OS): Love Babbar OS Placement Course for rapid fundamental review.
● Database Management Systems (DBMS): Love Babbar DBMS Interview Prep Video — prioritize the database scaling and architecture concepts at the end for system design rounds (mandatory).
● SQL: Focus on complex joins, nested subqueries, and window functions through randomized practice problems instead of memorizing static sheets.
● Computer Networks (CN): Core review of standard protocols, the OSI model, and basic TCP/IP mechanics.

Final Outcome
At the conclusion of the recruitment drive, 8 people were selected from our college to join MoneyView.
