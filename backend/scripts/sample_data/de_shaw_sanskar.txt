D. E. Shaw Placement Experience –
Sanskar Pandey (IT)

1. Eligibility
● CPI Criteria:
○ 7.0+ for Tech Branches
○ 8.0+ for Non-Tech Branches

2. Online Assessment (OA)
The OA consisted of two DSA coding questions of medium and hard difficulty. The questions required efficient solutions and a good understanding of data structures and algorithms, I don’t exactly remember the problem’s.

Preparation Tips:
● Practice medium-level DSA problems consistently.
● Focus on writing optimized solutions for medium one and for hard ones some-time even Brute works.

3. Technical Interview
The interview primarily focused on:
● DSA medium.
● An in-depth discussion of my projects.
● The tech stack I had used, why I chose those technologies, and where they are used in real-world applications.
● Design decisions, implementation details, and challenges faced during development.

My advice is to know every project on your resume thoroughly, including the architecture, tech stack, challenges faced, and possible improvements, along with cases in which my project will fail.

4. Preparation Tips
● Prioritize building strong DSA and problem-solving skills.
● Revise CS fundamentals (OOP, DBMS, OS, CN) based on the company you're applying to.
● Understand every detail of your projects instead of just building them.
● Since the role is testing-centric, it is beneficial to learn automation testing concepts and tools such as Selenium, Playwright, JUnit/TestNG, or similar testing frameworks.
● Work on your communication skills. Being able to explain your thought process, justify your decisions, and communicate confidently during interviews is just as important as solving the problem itself.

5. Final Advice
Strong problem-solving skills, a solid understanding of your projects, and good communication are the three things that help’s most during the process. For testing-oriented roles, having some exposure to automation testing tools and frameworks can also give you an edge.

Best of luck with your placements!
