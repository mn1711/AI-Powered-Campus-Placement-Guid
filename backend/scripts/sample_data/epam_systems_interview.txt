EPAM Systems – Brief Overview and Personal Experience

EPAM Systems is a service-based software company. In many campus placements, it typically requires a minimum CGPA of around 7.0 (this may vary by campus and hiring drive). At first, the company may seem very attractive because of its reputation and opportunities.

However, I want to share my personal experience. After joining as a Junior Software Engineer through campus recruitment, you are not immediately assigned to a project team. Instead, you may be allocated to different technology stacks such as Java, .NET, or others based on business requirements.

Even after clearing all the interview rounds, the learning continues during the internship. In my experience, the training was very demanding, and I felt that trainees were not always treated with enough respect. During the internship, there were two major interviews, each conducted about two and a half months apart. After that, there was a DSA test every month. I also felt that some Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) treated trainees very strictly and without much respect.

This reflects my personal experience and may not represent everyone’s experience at EPAM. After receiving a full-time offer, you must also clear client interviews before being assigned to a client project.
